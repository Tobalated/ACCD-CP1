EV Dashboard Design Process

Designed and documented by Oloruntoba Oni

Sketch Modeling Navigation Display Final Render Reflection

Initial Sketch & Concept

This sketch marks the starting point of the EV Dashboard design. I focused on achieving a smooth curve across the dash, a centered touchscreen, and ergonomic steering placement similar to modern Tesla interiors.

Modeling the Dashboard

The dashboard was modeled procedurally using a sinusoidal curvature and solidify modifier for realistic depth.

The steering wheel and touchscreen were placed for a natural driver’s view. Adjustments were made for scale and alignment.

Integrating the Navigation Screen

The central screen was textured with an emissive map image to simulate an active Waze-like navigation system. The blue route line and icons reflect real-time interface design inspiration.

Final Render

The final render used Cycles with area and point lights to achieve realistic shading, screen glow, and material reflection.

Reflection

This project taught me the importance of balancing form and function in automotive design. From 3D modeling to lighting, every step contributed to a believable in-car experience. The integration of UI design principles into physical modeling also deepened my understanding of interaction design.
