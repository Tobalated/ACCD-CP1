Complex Motion Rhythm – Animation Process

This page documents the evolution of a p5.js animation exploring complex motion rhythms: one shape moves in continuous trigonometric motion, while another reacts after a timed interval. Randomness and Perlin noise are used to create more organic forms and rhythms.
